01 / Assessment

A benchmark with a point of view

The project asks a valuable question that generic code-generation benchmarks miss: can an agent discover and use this team's real components, APIs and tokens? Keep that narrow focus. The static audit is a useful entry point; the behavioral benchmark is where the claim becomes evidence.

Strong foundations

Extracted ground truth, neutral task prompts, isolated workspaces, a worst-dimension gate and explicit missing measurements show care for the instrument. Regrading stored work and grounding written reports are particularly valuable.

The main risk

A precise-looking number can outlive its experimental conditions. Missing cells, incompatible scoring bases, changing fixtures and model aliases can make a confident comparison say more than the evidence supports.

The product opportunity

Help a maintainer get from an unconfigured system to a credible first finding, then identify the smallest change to test. That workflow is more valuable now than a longer list of supported channels.

My recommendation

Prioritize comparison validity and a trustworthy first run. Treat additional context channels, rankings and new score dimensions as experiments until they demonstrate incremental value and can be compared safely.

04 / Judgment calls

Where I differ from the current roadmap

The existing plan's principle, fix the instrument before measuring more, is right. I would tighten what counts as fixing it and delay a few attractive additions.

Keep and bring forward

  • Provenance (B1): expand beyond model and effort to task, grader, fixture and context identity. Unknown is a first-class value.
  • Mantine compile failure: investigate as fixture validity before quoting affected gates. Do not assume the correct fix until the cause is reproduced.
  • Judge coverage and context isolation: preserve the recent A1–A5 work and its evidence. The roadmap's initial state table predates those working-tree changes.

Change the proposal

  • B2: show spread and coverage, not automatic ties based on three reps.
  • D1: screenshots cannot supply all proposed DOM and narrow-viewport facts; capture new structured evidence and shadow it before gating.
  • C4: percentiles from a small convenience sample describe relative position, not validated readiness thresholds. Publish distributions first; validate labels against independent outcomes before recalibration.

Defer until evidence demands it

  • MCP, Code Connect and artifact quality heuristics: useful hypotheses, but version and vendor churn can turn superficial conformance into score inflation. Pilot their behavioral value before assigning score weight.
  • Batching and prompt caching: valuable when spend warrants it, but provider-specific semantics and pricing need verification; they do not repair a biased comparison.
  • Additional frameworks and agent families: postpone until fixture and policy differences can be made observable and comparable.

Protect the useful constraints

  • Keep the worst-dimension gate, system-agnostic configuration and separate agentic versus single-shot reporting.
  • Keep static and behavioral evidence distinct, null for genuinely unmeasurable checks, and report interpretation unconstrained by a prescribed conclusion.
  • Keep the GitHub-first distribution and offline test suite. Do not add a hosted product or package pipeline merely to make the roadmap look larger.
